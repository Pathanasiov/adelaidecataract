For referring practitioners

Referrals for cataract assessment can be sent by email at any time. Please include the patient’s contact details, relevant ocular and medical history, current medications and any recent imaging.

Referrals are acknowledged on receipt. Patients are contacted directly to arrange an appointment.

Fees

Adelaide Cataract is a private practice and does not bulk bill. The out-of-pocket cost for a new patient consultation is approximately $200 after the Medicare rebate.

Surgical fees are discussed at the appointment. Patients with private health insurance pay no gap for surgery.

Post-operative care is provided at no charge.

About the service

Cataract surgery is a planned procedure in which the clouded natural lens is removed and replaced with an intraocular lens. Whether surgery is appropriate, and which lens is suitable, is decided individually after examination and measurement.

All surgery carries risk. Risks and alternatives are discussed at consultation before any decision is made.
